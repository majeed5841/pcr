Frequently Asked Questions

Get quick answers to common questions about our repair services

General Repair Questions

Do I need an appointment?
No appointment necessary! We welcome walk-ins during business hours. However, calling ahead at (929) 767-8000 can help you avoid wait times.
How long do repairs take?
Most phone repairs are completed within 1-2 hours. Screen replacements take 30-60 minutes. Computer repairs vary from same-day to 2-3 days depending on the issue.
Do you offer warranty on repairs?
Yes! All repairs come with warranty. Screen repairs: 90 days, Battery replacements: 6 months, Other repairs: 30-90 days depending on service.
Is diagnostic free?
Yes, we provide free diagnostics on all devices. You'll know the exact problem and cost before any work begins.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Discover), debit cards, and digital payments.
Will I lose my data during repair?
Most repairs don't affect your data. However, we always recommend backing up your device before any repair as a precaution.

Phone Repair Questions

How much does screen repair cost?
iPhone screens: $99-$329, Samsung AMOLED: $149-$299, Other Android: $89-$199. Price varies by model. Call for exact quote.
Can you fix water damaged phones?
Yes! Bring it in immediately for best results. We specialize in water damage recovery with specialized cleaning and corrosion prevention.
Do you repair all phone brands?
Yes! iPhone, Samsung, Google Pixel, Motorola, LG, OnePlus, and virtually all smartphone brands.
How long does battery replacement take?
Most battery replacements are completed in 30-45 minutes while you wait.

Computer Repair Questions

Can you recover data from dead laptops?
In most cases, yes! We specialize in data recovery from failed hard drives and non-booting computers.
Do you fix MacBooks?
Yes! We repair all Apple computers including MacBook Pro, MacBook Air, iMac, and Mac Mini.
How much is virus removal?
Standard virus removal: $99-$149. Advanced malware/ransomware: $149-$249. Includes security optimization.
Should I upgrade or buy new computer?
Often upgrading with SSD and more RAM dramatically improves performance at fraction of new computer cost. Bring it in for assessment.
