Professional Phone Screen Repair in Brooklyn, NY

A cracked or broken phone screen is one of the most common issues we see at PCR Phone and Computer Repair. Whether you dropped your iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, or any other smartphone, our expert technicians can restore your device's display quickly and affordably.

We serve customers from Park Slope, Williamsburg, Downtown Brooklyn, Bushwick, Brooklyn Heights, and throughout NYC with same-day screen replacement services.

Symptoms That Indicate You Need Screen Repair

  • Visible cracks or shattered glass: Even small cracks can spread and worsen over time
  • Touch sensitivity issues: Screen not responding to touches or registering phantom touches
  • Display discoloration: Strange colors, lines, or dark spots on screen
  • Flickering display: Screen flickers or goes black intermittently
  • LCD damage: Black ink-like spots spreading across display

Our Screen Repair Process

  1. Assessment: Free diagnostic to evaluate screen damage and check for additional issues
  2. Parts Selection: We use premium quality replacement screens (OLED/AMOLED for compatible devices)
  3. Careful Disassembly: Technicians safely remove damaged screen without harming internal components
  4. Screen Installation: New screen installed with proper adhesive and calibration
  5. Testing: Comprehensive touch and display testing to ensure perfect functionality
  6. Quality Check: Final inspection before returning your device

Screen Repair FAQs

How long does screen replacement take?
Most phone screen replacements are completed within 30-60 minutes. Complex models with curved screens may take slightly longer. We keep popular screen models in stock for immediate service.
Will my data be safe during screen repair?
Yes! Screen replacement doesn't affect your data. Your photos, contacts, apps, and files remain intact. However, we always recommend regular backups as a precaution.
Do you offer warranty on screen repairs?
Absolutely. All screen repairs come with a 90-day warranty covering defects in parts and workmanship. If you experience any issues with the replacement screen, we'll fix it at no charge.
Can you fix phones with severely shattered screens?
Yes, we can replace even completely shattered screens. Bring your phone in carefully to avoid glass pieces falling out. In rare cases of extreme damage, additional repairs might be needed.
What's the difference between LCD and OLED screen replacement?
OLED screens offer better color accuracy, deeper blacks, and lower power consumption. We use OLED/AMOLED screens for phones that originally came with them. LCD is used for budget-friendly options on compatible models.
