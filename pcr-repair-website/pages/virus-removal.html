Signs Your Computer Has a Virus

  • Slow Performance: Computer takes forever to start or programs run slowly
  • Pop-up Ads: Excessive pop-ups, even when not browsing
  • Browser Hijacking: Homepage changed without permission, new toolbars
  • Security Software Disabled: Antivirus won't turn on or update
  • Strange Behavior: Programs crash, files disappear, unknown programs installed
  • Email Issues: Contacts receive spam from your email without your knowledge

Virus Removal FAQs

How long does virus removal take?
Most virus removals are completed within 2-4 hours. Severe infections may require overnight service. We'll give you an accurate timeline after initial diagnosis.
Will I lose my files during virus removal?
In most cases, no. We take precautions to preserve your files. However, we always recommend regular backups. In rare cases of severe corruption, some files may be affected.
Can you remove ransomware?
We can remove ransomware infections, but file recovery depends on the ransomware type. Some variants encrypt files permanently. Bring your computer in immediately for best chances.
Do you provide antivirus software?
Yes! We install and configure reputable antivirus software as part of our service. We can recommend free or paid options based on your needs.
How can I prevent future virus infections?
Keep antivirus updated, avoid suspicious emails/links, download only from trusted sources, enable firewall, and keep Windows/Mac updated. We provide detailed prevention tips after service.
