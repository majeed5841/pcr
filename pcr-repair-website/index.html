Frequently Asked Questions

Get quick answers to common questions about our repair services

How long does a typical phone repair take?
Most phone repairs are completed within 1-2 hours. Screen replacements typically take 30-60 minutes, while battery replacements take about 30 minutes. More complex repairs like water damage may require 24-48 hours for proper drying and testing.
Do you offer warranty on repairs?
Yes! All our repairs come with a warranty. Screen repairs include a 90-day warranty, battery replacements have a 6-month warranty, and other repairs vary from 30-90 days depending on the service performed. We stand behind our work.
Can you recover data from a broken phone?
In most cases, yes! We offer professional data recovery services for phones that won't turn on, have water damage, or have broken screens. Bring your device in for a free diagnostic to determine the best recovery approach.
Do I need an appointment?
No appointment necessary! We welcome walk-ins during business hours. However, if you prefer, you can call ahead at (929) 767-8000 to check wait times or schedule a specific time slot.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, and digital payments. Payment is due upon completion of the repair service.
